The Space

Cosy, private and
ready to party.

The bar is a semi private, partitioned room set apart from the main café, so your guests get their own space while the kitchen keeps the food coming.

Pale wood, warm light, good company

Pendant lights, backlit bottle shelves and framed artwork give the room a relaxed, lived in feel. It works just as well for a quiet seated gathering as it does for a full standing reception.

Turntables and a dance floor

Two Technics decks, a mixer, a built in PA and a small dance floor. Bring your own DJ or ask the venue to book one.
